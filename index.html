CLINICAL OPTOMETRY PROTOCOL

1. Target Gaze: Look directly into the camera lens, not at your own face on screen. This establishes true infinity/far gaze and prevents excessive convergence.

2. Working Distance: Maintain an arm's distance of roughly 45 cm to 60 cm from the webcam in good ambient lighting.

3. Pose Leveling: Keep your head level. The system blocks sampling if yaw or tilt exceeds 3.5° to prevent trigonometric parallax error.

4. Auto Lock-In: Once alignment is optimal, keep steady for 2.5 seconds. The system accumulates 30 filtered biometric frames to synthesize your Far PD, OD, and OS values.
